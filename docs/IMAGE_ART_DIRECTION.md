## Final attempted prompt (verbatim)

Premium comic-book cover illustration for a creative frontend developer portfolio. A single masked superhero upper-body portrait centered, costume split exactly vertically: left half Spider-Man red mask with black web lines and luminous white eye, right half Batman dark charcoal cowl with pointed ear and white eye. One fused symbolic face, no real human reference. Arms relaxed, powerful quiet pose. Off-white paper backdrop, large vermilion disk behind head, distant city skyline below. Thick black ink linework, red and ivory screen-print halftone, sparse electric blue accents. Sophisticated graphic-novel cover with beautifully detailed texture, strong silhouette, dramatic editorial composition. Vertical 4:5 image, full head including bat ear and shoulders with ample margin. No lettering, no text, no watermark.
