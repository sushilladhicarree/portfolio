## Content constraints

No invented experience counts, launch dates, project performance improvements, testimonials, ratings, clients or awards. The existing repository’s email address was retained; the three supplied social links replace dummy destinations.

The small product previews are original interface illustrations. They are labeled as portfolio interpretations and do not claim to reproduce the shipped UI.

## Creative direction

The strongest personal through-line is “Draw. Design. Develop.” The portfolio treats comic imagery as a visual frame for real frontend work, using readable project descriptions, explicit contributions, a clear contact route, restrained motion and optional playful interactions.
